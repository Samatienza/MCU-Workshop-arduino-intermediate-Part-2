# Welcome to the Embedded Audio & Synthesizer Workshop!

This intermediate workshop bridges the physics of digital audio with embedded microcontroller programming. Building on foundational concepts like digital I/O, polling loops, and arrays, you will explore how sound waves are mathematically modeled and how microcontrollers generate and sculpt audio in real time.

Just as video games rely on polling loops and timers to respond to player input, digital synthesizers continuously read physical potentiometers and pushbuttons to shape oscillators, filters, and envelopes.

By the end of this 2-hour workshop, you will have tested, programmed, and customized your own playable 6-key hardware synthesizer and song sequencer!

# Required Materials

* 1x Seeeduino Nano
* 1x Custom Synthesizer PCB
* 1x USB-C to USB-A Cable
* 1x Speaker Cabinet
* **Software:** Audacity & Arduino IDE installed
* **Starter Code Folders:** `PCB_CONNECTION_CHECKER`, `SYNTH_BASE`, `SYNTH_SEQUENCER`
