# Submission 1

Due Friday October 2<sup>nd</sup> by 11:59 pm

## Carboard Cut CAD 

As described in the Day 1 activity, you are expected to model your
12”x12” cardboard cut pattern in AutoCAD, including dimensions, as
described in the activity.

As a group, submit a PDF of this to the appropriate Tron Days dropbox on
Crowdmark. If you have other drawings, feel free to include them on
separate pages.

- Note that all of your cutouts will have to fit on a 12x12” sheet of
  cardboard, since that’s all you get.

- For dimensions, baseline dimensioning doesn’t matter too much for this
  assignment, and not everything (such as weird curves) needs to be
  dimensioned. The dimensions are just to get a general sense of how big
  your parts are.

- Your cutting lines should be object lines, and folding lines should
  use hidden lines.

- You must have a title block (the size/shape of the block doesn’t
  matter for this time) with the name of the speaker, the names of your
  group members, and the scale of your drawing.

Grading Scheme /3

- Correct size, fits on cardboard sheet.

- All necessary dimensions included.

- Clear and legible submission, with names of group members.
